# Guidance when you need it

**Version:** 1.9.0
**Release Date:** TBD

## What's New

### Get a quick introduction

Brief, skippable walkthroughs point out the controls and shortcuts in search and the Tab Manager. New and returning users can get oriented, then replay either walkthrough from Options whenever they like.

### Discover more at your own pace

Tips appear gradually after you have spent time with Tabby and repeated a relevant action. See how to make your preferred window behavior the default, recognize windows, and work with selections and groups. Change a setting directly from a tip, with Undo if you change your mind. Guidance stays quiet after walkthroughs and is limited to one automatic tip per day.

### Keep every tip within reach

Browse all tips and walkthroughs in Options from the start, including tips you have already seen. Turn off automatic tips whenever you prefer.
