# Restore closed items

**Version:** 1.8.0
**Release Date:** TBD

## What's New

### Browse recently closed tabs and windows

Use the view switcher at the top of the Tab Manager to browse recently closed tabs and windows in their own pane. Restore a single tab or preview and restore an entire window, then return to your open tabs where you left off. Chrome keeps up to 25 closed tabs and windows combined, so this is recent history rather than a permanent archive.

### Clearer closed-window search results

Search shows each closed window once, even when several of its tabs match, and makes it clear that restoring it brings back the entire window.
