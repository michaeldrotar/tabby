## What's New

### Keep favorites close

Choose a Chrome bookmark folder or create Tabby Favorites to keep useful pages at the top of the Tab Manager. Add the current page, browse folders, and remove bookmarks while keeping everything in sync with Chrome. Open a favorite to return to its matching tab in the current window, or use modifiers for a fresh tab or window.
