# Seasonal icons

**Version:** 1.5.0
**Release Date:** 2026-10-15

## What's New

### Choose seasonal icons

Choose an icon for each seasonal occasion, disable any individual occasion, or turn seasonal icons off entirely. Tabby shows each selected icon during its seasonal window.

### See seasonal icons across Tabby

During its seasonal window, the selected icon appears on Tabby's toolbar and in the Options page header.

### Choose Easter calendar and Thanksgiving region

Choose Western or Orthodox Easter, or use the browser region. Set Thanksgiving to your browser region, the United States, or Canada.

### More room in the sidebar

The "Scroll to active" button has been removed from the sidebar, leaving more room for windows. Search and Settings remain together, and automatic scrolling to the active tab continues when needed.
