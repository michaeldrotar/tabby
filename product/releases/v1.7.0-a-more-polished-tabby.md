# A more polished Tabby

**Version:** 1.7.0
**Release Date:** 2026-10-25

Drag and drop, richer themes, smoother keyboard navigation, and smarter search.

## What's New

### Put tabs where you want them

Drag tabs, selections, and whole groups to reorder them or move them between windows. Clear feedback helps you see what you're moving and where it can go.

### Follow your moved tabs

Moving tabs to another window brings that window and its Tab Manager forward, ready to continue where you left off.

### Coordinated color themes

Choose from fifteen complete looks, from calm neutrals with subtle headers to combinations of coral, mint, plum, lavender, and gold. Each brings matching backgrounds, controls, and selections to light and dark mode, so Tabby feels more personal with a single choice.

New installs and extension updates start with Porcelain, following your system’s light or dark mode. Choose another theme in Options at any time.

### Simpler, easier-to-read options

Larger text and clearer headings make Options easier to scan. Tonal themes appear first, with simpler previews to help you choose a look. Preferences are changed individually, keeping your other choices intact.

### Clearer tab groups

Tab groups keep a single color bar, making their color easier to recognize when you switch tabs.

### Smoother keyboard navigation

Move naturally between tabs, groups, windows, and window editing with more consistent keyboard controls.

### Keep your place after closing a tab

Keep working with the keyboard after closing a tab. Focus follows the new active tab, or stays beside the tab you closed when it was inactive.

### Close tabs with a middle click

Middle-click a tab in the Tab Manager to close it without switching to it or changing your selection of other tabs.

### Add a tab where you need it

Create a new tab directly below an existing tab, keeping it in the same group when applicable.

### Search that remembers your choices

Repeat a search to bring the destinations you recently chose toward the top, whether they are commands, windows, tab groups, or pages. Better result ordering helps you work through similar tabs, and keyboard selection stays on the same result as more matches arrive.
