# Drag and drop tab organization

**Version:** 1.7.0
**Release Date:** TBD

## What's New

### Put tabs where you want them

Drag tabs, selections, and whole groups to reorder them, with automatic scrolling through long lists. Drop on a window in the picker to move tabs there, ready to arrange in their new home.

### Follow your moved tabs

Moving tabs to an existing or new window brings that window forward and opens its Tab Manager with the moved tabs selected and the first one active and in view.

### Clearer colors and focus

Distinct light palettes and brighter dark surfaces give your chosen colors more presence. Creation rows match the tabs and windows around them. Active tabs and windows keep their color on hover, and keyboard focus outlines stay out of the way when clicking with the mouse.

### Try colors beside your tabs

Adjust your theme, palettes, and accent strength from a compact drawer that stays open while you try different colors and use your tabs.

### Reach more with the arrow keys

Use Tab to move between sections and through window editing, with arrow keys to choose icons and colors and reach New window. Expand and collapse groups with Right and Left, with clearer group focus and no lingering single-tab selection when moving to New tab.
