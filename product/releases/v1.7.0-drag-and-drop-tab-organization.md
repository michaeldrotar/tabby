# Drag and drop tab organization

**Version:** 1.7.0
**Release Date:** TBD

## What's New

### Put tabs where you want them

Drag tabs, selections, and whole groups to reorder them, with automatic scrolling through long lists. Drop on a window in the picker to move tabs there, ready to arrange in their new home.

### Follow your moved tabs

Moving tabs to an existing or new window brings that window forward and opens its Tab Manager with the moved tabs selected and the first one active and in view.

### Try six Tab Manager looks

Compare six Tab Manager designs from the Design button, starting with Refined. Refined pairs the current tab layout and interaction styling with matching lighter window bars and a softly tinted window list. Background palettes give surfaces a clearer cool or warm tone, while foreground palettes give titles and supporting text a distinct tone. Switch between light and dark appearance, and adjust surface contrast and theme colors while the preview stays open. Design previews reset when Tab Manager reloads; appearance choices save to Settings.
