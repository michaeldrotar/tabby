## What's New

### Your recent downloads beside your tabs

Switch between Tabs and Downloads at the top of the Tab Manager to find your latest files. Select downloads individually or together, then right-click for the actions you need. Open files, reveal them in their folder, and pause, resume, or cancel downloads without leaving the sidebar. Access is optional, and removing a download from history keeps the file on your device.
