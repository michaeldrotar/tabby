# The Launch 🚀

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Release Date:** 2025-11-24

**Tabby is here!**
I am thrilled to introduce Tabby, your new browser experience. Tabby transforms how you navigate Chrome, turning your browser into a keyboard-centric command line for maximum productivity.

## ✨ What's New

### 🔍 The Omnibar (Cmd+E / Alt+E)

Stop hunting for tabs. Press one shortcut to instantly search everything:

- **Universal Search**: Find any open tab, bookmark, or history item in milliseconds.
- **Web Search**: Type your query and jump straight to Google results.
- **Commands**: Run browser actions like opening Settings or managing extensions without touching your mouse.

### 🗂️ Tab Manager (Cmd+Shift+E / Alt+Shift+E)

Bring order to the chaos. My new side panel gives you a bird's-eye view of your browsing session:

- **Multi-Window Support**: See all your open windows in one clean list.
- **Tab Groups**: Visualize and organize your tab groups effortlessly.
- **Fast Switching**: Jump between tabs and windows with a click.

### 🔒 Privacy First

I built Tabby with a "Privacy by Design" philosophy:

- **Local Data Processing**: Searches across your tabs, bookmarks, and history run locally on your device. Choosing **Search Google** sends your query to Google in the search URL.

### 🐞 Bug Fixes

- **Shortcut Bindings**: Fixed the default shortcut bindings to use Windows keys for non-Mac users. **(v1.0.1)**
