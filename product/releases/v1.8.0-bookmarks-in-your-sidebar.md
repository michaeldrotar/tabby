# Bookmarks in your sidebar

**Version:** 1.8.0
**Release Date:** TBD

## What's New

### Your bookmarks, close at hand

Switch between tabs and bookmarks from the bottom bar. Browse, filter, and organize Chrome bookmarks, or select several to open, move, or delete together. Open a folder's bookmarks together, or turn its folder structure into windows and tab groups.

### Save a window for later

Bookmark a window and restore its tabs in order, including group names and colors, whenever you need them again.
